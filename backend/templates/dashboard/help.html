🎓 Getting Started

How do I join a class?
Go to Classrooms from the sidebar. Enter the class code provided by your teacher in the "Join Class" field and click Join. You'll immediately see the class in your dashboard.
How do I submit an assignment?
Navigate to Assignments, click on the assignment you want to submit, and use the submission form at the bottom. You can type your answer or upload a file.
How do I check my attendance?
Students can view their attendance from My Attendance in the sidebar. You can filter by class and see your overall attendance percentage.
How do I send a message?
Go to Messages → Compose. Select the recipient, type your subject and message, then click Send.
How are XP and badges earned?
You earn XP automatically by attending classes (10 XP each), submitting assignments (25 XP each), and scoring well on exams. Badges are awarded for milestones like streaks, perfect attendance, and high scores. Check the Achievements page for details.

👨‍🏫 For Teachers

How do I create a class?
Go to Classrooms and click Create Class. Fill in the class name, subject, and department. A unique class code will be generated for students to join.
How do I mark attendance?
Go to Mark Attendance, select a class and date, then mark each student as Present, Absent, or Late and save.
How do I create & grade assignments?
Go to Assignments → Create Assignment. After students submit, click on the assignment to view submissions. Enter marks and feedback for each student.

📧 Contact Support

If you can't find the answer you're looking for, please contact the system administrator or use the Messages feature to reach out to your teacher or admin directly.
